Create your own animated gradient background

  1. Pick your colours. Start from a palette, tap a swatch to change it, or pull colours from a photo.
  2. Play with the controls. Change the blob size, edges, wobble, speed and grain. The preview updates as you go.
  3. Save it. Download a still image or record a seamless video loop, sized for screens, stories or posts.
